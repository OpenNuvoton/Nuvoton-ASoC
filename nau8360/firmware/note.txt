=======================================================================
WARNING: PREVENT SPEAKER DAMAGE WHEN LOADING KCS FIRMWARE
=======================================================================
The NAU83G60 is a stereo 30W+30W smart amplifier with a DSP running the 
KCS (Klippel Controlled Sound) algorithm. 

Please double-check your speaker's maximum power rating (Wattage) before 
applying the KCS firmware. Using a high-power firmware profile on 
low-wattage speakers will cause the speakers to burn out or break.

To prevent permanent hardware damage, please contact Nuvoton for 
speaker-specific firmware tuning and validation.
