The NAU85L42 High-Performance Quad-Channel Audio ADC Driver

This device supports I2C only.

Required properties:

  - compatible : "nuvoton,nau8542"

  - reg : the I2C address of the device.

Example:

codec: nau8540@1c {
       #sound-dai-cells = <0>;
       compatible = "nuvoton,nau8540";
       reg = <0x1c>;
       status = "okay";
};
